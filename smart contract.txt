What to Store on Blockchain
✅ Hashes of medical records (not the actual data)

✅ Access permissions (who can view what)

✅ Audit logs (who accessed when)

✅ Insurance claim status

✅ Medication history (track prescriptions)

❌ Actual medical data (too large, privacy concerns)
